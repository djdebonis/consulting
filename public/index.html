About

I am a researcher and science communicator interested in how people understand complex information and use it to change their lives. My work sits at the intersection of biomedical research, linguistics, health education, and writing.

At the Department of Biomedical Informatics at CU Anschutz, I helped communicate work in bioinformatics, clinical informatics, artificial intelligence, and research discovery. I also bring a practical health perspective as a certified personal trainer and health coach, with an interest in helping people build sustainable relationships with movement, recovery, and everyday health.

Marketing is part of my toolkit, but it is not the whole story. I use strategy, writing, data, and digital systems to make useful ideas more accessible and more actionable.
