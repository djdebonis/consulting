I’m a marketing strategist and writer with a background in research and language. I’m interested in how people make sense of what they read, and how that understanding shapes what they do.

At CU Anschutz, I write about biomedical research. Through Antilo Design, I work with businesses on their marketing and websites. Health, language, and human behavior keep finding their way into my independent writing, too.

Areas of practice

What I work on

Marketing & digital strategy

I help businesses explain what they offer, then carry that work into websites, product pages, and email. Projects often involve brand positioning, SEO, and e-commerce.

Scientific communication

I write about biomedical informatics, health, and AI: what researchers are studying, why it matters, and what the evidence can and cannot tell us.

Writing & research
