## Biomedical research communication

**University of Colorado Anschutz Medical Campus · Science communication**

At CU Anschutz, I write about biomedical informatics, research software, and artificial intelligence. These articles cover uncertainty in health AI and software for studying biological images.

- [Can AI Say “I Don’t Know”? Understanding Uncertainty in AI](https://news.cuanschutz.edu/dbmi/can-ai-say-i-dont-know-healthcare) — reporting on uncertainty in health AI.
- [From Microscopes to Treatments: The Cytomining Ecosystem](https://news.cuanschutz.edu/dbmi/cytomining-open-source-image-based-profiling) — an introduction to tools for studying biological images.

[Browse my CU Anschutz author archive](https://news.cuanschutz.edu/dbmi/author/david-debonis).

## Local research and useful content

**TGB Flooring · Through Antilo Design**

For TGB Flooring, I developed company and project stories and content for the areas the business serves. I also compared 117 Highlands Ranch home listings for a research resource. The published findings explain the methods and limitations; an association in the listings does not establish cause and effect.

[Read the TGB Flooring case study](https://antilodesign.com/work/tgb-flooring/).
